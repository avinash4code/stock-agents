## Reliance Industries (NSE: RELIANCE) — News Roundup (Oct 21–27, 2026)

**Overview:** Coverage is dominated by the Jio Platforms IPO listing and a fresh ₹1,000-crore-plus order win at Jamnagar, while the RIL stock itself drifted lower in cautious, low-volume trade.

**Key developments**

- **Jio Platforms IPO is fully subscribed in record time.** The ₹30,213-crore issue was fully subscribed within 100 minutes of opening on Oct 21 and closed Oct 23, ending 22.2x subscribed with total demand above ₹7.4 lakh crore, described as India's fastest-ever full subscription and largest public offer. Up to ₹27,500 crore of proceeds is earmarked for repaying borrowings. (NewsBytes, Oct 26, 2026; LatestLY, Oct 27, 2026)
- **Jio lists on Wednesday; grey market points to a strong debut.** The latest GMP of ₹248.5 implies an indicative listing price of about ₹1,367.5 per share — roughly 22.2% above the IPO price band of ₹1,065–₹1,119 — with listing on NSE/BSE at 10:00 am. (NewsBytes, Oct 26, 2026)
- **Analysts cite scale and margins as the Jio bull case.** Anand Rathi's Narendra Solanki pointed to strong profitability, a scalable tech platform and Jio's 533.3 million subscribers with a 49.8% share of wireless broadband; another analyst noted 7,220 patent applications underpinning a 51.91% EBITDA margin, with growth expected from tariff hikes, data usage and JioFiber/JioAirFiber. Risks flagged: telecom regulation, spectrum needs, technological disruption and cybersecurity. (NewsBytes, Oct 26, 2026)
- **VA Tech Wabag bags a >₹1,000-crore "mega" order from RIL.** The contract covers a desalination plant (8,400 m3/hr, expandable to 12,600 m3/hr or 302 MLD) and a 3,000 m3/hr remineralisation plant at Jamnagar, executed in phases over 25 months. It follows a September "medium" order from RIL for an effluent treatment plant at the Dhirubhai Ambani Green Energy Giga Complex. Wabag shares rose ~8% on the news. (CNBC-TV18, Oct 27, 2026)
- **RIL stock edged lower amid broad market caution.** Shares traded at ₹1,195.90, down 0.49% from ₹1,201.80, on subdued volume of ~920,000 shares; the dip was attributed to lingering concerns over elevated crude oil prices, geopolitical tensions and Fed policy uncertainty, plus reports of a 4% decline in the O2C segment in the recent quarter on logistical issues and rising costs. (LatestLY, Oct 27, 2026)
